The Mission

Kenya · March 2027

In March 2027, I'm traveling to Kenya with a team partnering with trusted Christian leaders there. We'll be serving Utawala and the neighboring villages through evangelism, church ministry, humanitarian outreach and leadership development, ministering to men, women, teenagers and children.

The bigger goal is to strengthen the local churches and leaders we serve alongside, so the ministry keeps going long after our team comes home. Our Kenyan partners often bring several churches and communities together, so we're preparing to serve everyone from kids to pastors.

This first run of Cross Spurs Co. shirts helps fund and prepare for this trip.

Questions

Good to know

When will my shirt arrive?

Preorders close November 30, and printing starts after that. You'll get an email with your ship or pickup date as soon as it's set. If your order is ever delayed, we'll let you know right away, and you can choose a full refund instead of waiting.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is $6. Local pickup in McAllen, Edinburg or Mission, TX is free. You'll choose shipping or local pickup at checkout. If you pick up, we'll contact you to set a time.

How does sizing run?

Both tees are the same unisex classic fit, which runs like standard men's sizing. Men: order your usual size. Women: most size down one from your usual women's size for a closer fit, or keep your usual size for a relaxed, oversized look.

Will my shirt look exactly like the photos?

The photos are mockups of the final design. Fabric texture and print color can look slightly different in person and from screen to screen.

How does my purchase support the mission trip?

This first run helps fund and prepare for my mission trip to Kenya in March 2027. Shirt purchases aren't tax-deductible donations.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. You can cancel for a full refund any time before preorders close on November 30. After that your shirt goes into production. If your order is delayed, you can still choose a full refund.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payment runs through Square's secure checkout. We never see or store your card details.

Questions about my order?

Email with the name on your order and we'll take care of you.
